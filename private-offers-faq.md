# FAQ about private offers
{: #private-offers-faq}

Frequently asked questions about private offers cover how to identify clients, track offer status, and manage discount changes. To find all FAQs for {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}}, see the [FAQ library](/docs/faqs).
{: shortdesc}

## How do I identify the client who should receive the private offer?
{: #faq-private-offers-client-id}
{: faq}

You must provide the client's name and country when you create a private offer. The client's {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} account ID is optional. For more information, see [Offering discounts through private offers](/docs/sell?topic=sell-private-offers#private-offers-create).

## How will I know whether my private offer has been approved or rejected?
{: #faq-private-offers-status}
{: faq}

You receive email notifications when the status of your private offer changes. You can also check the current status of any offer at any time in Partner Center.

## What happens if the client wants a different discount than I originally offered?
{: #faq-private-offers-changes}
{: faq}

If the client requests a different discount, you receive an email notification. The requested changes are also visible in Partner Center, where you can review and either approve the changes or propose other changes.

## How do I create a private offer?
{: #faq-private-offers-create}
{: faq}

You can create a private offer for a client directly in Partner Center. For step-by-step instructions, see [Creating a private offer for a client](/docs/sell?topic=sell-private-offers#private-offers-create).
