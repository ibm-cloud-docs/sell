# Publishing your agent to the {{site.data.keyword.wxorchestrate_short}} and {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalogs
{: #agent-publish}
{: toc-content-type="tutorial"}
{: toc-completion-time="5m"}

This tutorial walks you through how to publish your agent to the {{site.data.keyword.wxorchestrate_short}} and {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalogs. By completing this tutorial, you submit your publishing request and publish your agent to both catalogs.
{: shortdesc}

{{_include-segments/notes.md}}

## Before you begin
{: #agent-publish-prereqs}

Before you can publish your agent, make sure to complete all onboarding tasks:

1. [Register your agent](/docs/sell?topic=sell-agent-register).
1. [Define your agent details](/docs/sell?topic=sell-agent-define).
1. [Onboard a broker](/docs/sell?topic=sell-broker-agent-onboard).
1. [Add a pricing plan](/docs/sell?topic=sell-agent-pricing).
1. [Submit your agent details](/docs/sell?topic=sell-agent-onboard).

## Submit your publishing request
{: #agent-publish-request}
{: step}

Before your agent can be published to the catalog, {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} reviews your submission to verify that all required details are complete and accurate, including your product name, catalog entry, pricing plan, support experience, and agent package.

1. In the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console, click the **Navigation menu** icon ![Navigation Menu icon](../icons/icon_hamburger.svg "Navigation menu") > **Partner Center** > **My products**.
1. Select the agent that you're onboarding.
1. Click **Request approval**.
1. In the **Select the version you want to submit for publishing approval** dialog, select **I confirm that my company is authorized to use all materials**.
1. Click **Request approval**.

After you submit, the onboarding checklist updates to show **Approval pending**. The {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} team reviews your agent package structure, metadata, and evaluation results and gets back to you.

If updates are required, you receive a separate email with details about what needs to be changed. After you address all feedback, you can submit another publishing request.
{: note}

## Publish your agent
{: #agent-publish-submit}
{: step}

When all product details are approved, the **Publish to {{site.data.keyword.wxorchestrate_short}}** button appears at the top of the page. Click **Publish to {{site.data.keyword.wxorchestrate_short}}** to submit your agent for publishing.

After you click the button, a **Publish request sent** confirmation appears. Your request to publish your agent to the {{site.data.keyword.wxorchestrate_short}} catalog has been sent. After your agent is published there, it is automatically published to the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog as well.

Publishing is not immediate. Your agent goes through a background processing period that takes approximately one month to complete. You receive an email notification when your agent is live.
{: important}

## Next steps
{: #agent-publish-next}

After you receive the email notification that your agent is live, verify that it appears in the {{site.data.keyword.wxorchestrate_short}} and [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalogs](https://cloud.ibm.com/catalog){: external} by searching for the name of your agent.
